## ✍️ Architecture & technical writing

[Data First, GenAI Second: Building AI-Ready Data on AWS](https://builder.aws.com/content/3J5AXToJRysJjuV39GAWCt1XKUw/data-first-genai-second-building-ai-ready-data-on-aws)

Part of **Building a Data & AI Platform on AWS**. The article presents the data-foundation-first approach behind an academic regulatory-intelligence use case. The architecture narrative connects Amazon S3, AWS Glue and Amazon Athena with a proposed Amazon Bedrock/RAG layer; publication of a design does not by itself establish deployment of every component.
